# Refund & Pricing Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-01. Owner: Finance Ops. This document is the ONLY source of truth for figures quoted to customers._

## Plans (monthly list price, per seat)
- Starter: $12 per seat / month
- Growth: $29 per seat / month
- Enterprise: custom pricing — never quote a number; route to Sales.
- Annual billing: 20% discount vs. monthly list price.

## Refunds
- Monthly plans: full refund if requested within 14 days of the charge.
- Annual plans: prorated refund for unused full months if cancelled within the first 60 days; no refund after 60 days.
- Duplicate or erroneous charges: always refunded in full, regardless of timing.
- Refunds post to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.

## Credits & goodwill
- Support agents may issue a goodwill credit of up to $50 per account per quarter without approval.
- Anything above $50 requires a Support Lead approval; do not promise it to the customer in advance.

## Outage SLA credits (Growth and Enterprise only)
- Monthly uptime below 99.9%: 10% service credit on that month's invoice.
- Monthly uptime below 99.0%: 25% service credit on that month's invoice.

## Things we never promise
- Price locks or future pricing.
- Refunds outside the windows above (escalate to Support Lead instead).
