# Tasklane Support — Tone of Voice

We sound like a capable colleague who happens to know the product inside out: **warm, direct, plain-spoken, and accountable.**

## The five rules

1. **Lead with the answer.** The first sentence tells the customer what's happening or what we did. Empathy comes second, in one short clause, and never takes up a whole paragraph.
2. **Own it.** If we broke something, say "we" and say sorry once: "That's on us, sorry." Don't write passive constructions like "mistakes were made" or "an issue occurred".
3. **Plain words, short sentences.** Aim for an average of 20 words or fewer per sentence. No jargon unless the customer used it first. Spell out what to click: **Settings → Billing**.
4. **Specific next step.** Every reply ends with what happens next and who does it: "I've sent this to our billing team; you'll hear from them within one business day." Promise only timelines that appear in the policy doc or the routing SLA.
5. **Human sign-off.** First name + "Tasklane Support". No corporate signature blocks.

## Formatting
- Greeting: "Hi {first name}," (or "Hi there," if the name is unknown). Never "Dear Customer" or "Greetings".
- 3–6 short paragraphs maximum. Use numbered steps for any instructions longer than two steps.
- No exclamation marks, except at most one in a genuinely celebratory moment.
- No emoji.
- Money is always written in digits with a currency symbol ($29), never in words.

## Banned phrases
- we apologize for any inconvenience
- sorry for the inconvenience
- please be advised
- per our policy
- as per
- kindly
- do the needful
- unfortunately, we are unable
- rest assured
- we value your business
- dear customer
- at this time
- we guarantee
- price lock
- lock in your price

## Before / after

**Before:** "Dear Customer, we apologize for any inconvenience. Per our policy, refunds are not possible at this time. Rest assured we value your business."

**After:** "Hi Priya, I can't refund this one: the annual plan's refund window closed 60 days after purchase, and you're at day 94. What I *can* do is move you to monthly billing from your next renewal so you're never locked in again. Want me to set that up?

— Sam, Tasklane Support"
